# Design Standard: Warm Scandinavian / Kinfolk (43–44 m² Flips)

> Master visual standard for Khrushchev / Brezhnevka 2-room apartment flips (e.g. Series 1-447, 43–44 m²).
> Source Case: Realized flip project by Anastasia Kamaeva (44 m² Khrushchev, Ekaterinburg / Divan.ru).

---

## 1. Why this style wins for Flipping (Unit Economics)

- **High Perceived Value at Low Baseline Cost:** Simple base finishes (smooth warm greige paint/wallpaper, standard light oak laminate, white panelled doors) elevated by strategic high-contrast black accents (sconces, slim handles, framed mirrors) photograph like an expensive architectural project.
- **Mass Appeal (Broadest Liquidity):** Avoids cold hospital-like white-on-gray sterility. Warm oat/creamy undertones evoke comfort and light, appealing to 90%+ of family and couple buyers in regional markets (e.g. Tula).
- **Zero Complex Wet Trades:** No complicated ceiling multi-levels, no heavy wall demolition.

---

## 2. Core Color Code & Material Matrix

| Surface / Element | Specification / Tone | Execution Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Walls (Base)** | Warm Oat / Greige / Cream (RAL 7047 / Tikkurila F497) | Matte washable paint or smooth non-woven wallpaper. Softens low light in Soviet brick buildings. |
| **Flooring** | Natural Nordic Light Oak (Class 33, 8–10 mm, 4V micro-bevel) | Seamless single-contour layout across entire flat. Zero yellow/orange tint. |
| **Baseboards & Doors** | White semi-matte panelled doors + 80 mm high white MDF/duropolymer baseboards | Defines crisp room boundaries and masks minor floor/wall unevenness. |
| **Metal Accents** | Matte Black (RAL 9005) | Minimalist cylinder pendants, swing-arm wall sconces, door lever handles, kitchen mixer tap. |
| **Textiles & Staging** | Natural unbleached linen, herringbone wool throws, oatmeal bouclé/textured weave | Adds tactile layering for listing photos. |

## 3. Room-by-Room Technical & Layout Solutions

### Living Room (18–19 m²)
- **Layout:** Straight 3-seater sofa (2.2–2.4 m) in textured beige/gray weave placed opposite the continuous TV wall (sealed dark room entrance).
- **Lighting & Electrical:**
  - Above sofa: Swing-arm black wall sconce (220V cable drop at **160 cm** height).
  - Sofa sides: Sockets at **30 cm** for floor lamps and phone chargers.
  - TV Zone: Sockets (3×220V + RJ45) concealed behind 55–65" display at **110 cm** height; lower sockets at **30 cm** for media console.

### Bedroom (12–13 m²) & Walk-in Closet
- **Layout:** 160×200 cm bed centered on the long wall with minimalist light headboard. Dark room doorway relocated to open from the bedroom into a 2.5–3 m² walk-in dressing room.
- **Lighting & Electrical:**
  - Bedside: 3-gang block (2×220V + light switch) at **65–70 cm** height above floating oak nightstands.
  - Sconce/Pendant: Ceiling-dropped minimalist black cylinder pendants or directional wall lamps over each nightstand.

### Kitchen (6.0 m² with Gas)
- **Layout:** Straight or compact L-shaped modular kitchen along utility wall with light cashmere/white matte slab doors and light oak butcher-block worktop.
- **Gas Riser & Backsplash:** Gas pipe painted in wall tone. Backsplash in vertical white ceramic subway tile (matt/gloss) with subtle gray grout. Sockets on backsplash at **105 cm** height.
- **Upper Cabinets:** Open oak display shelf on black brackets or ventilated cabinet enclosing gas heater.
- **Dining Area:** 70–80 cm round white table by window with vintage/curved wooden chairs.

### Bathroom (2.9–3.0 m² Combined)
- **Finishes:** 60×60 cm rectified porcelain tiles in light travertine / Calacatta marble tone.
- **Fixtures:** 150 cm acrylic tub with glass screen, 60 cm floating oak vanity with undermount basin, round LED backlit anti-fog mirror (220V drop at **160 cm**), matte black shower column.
